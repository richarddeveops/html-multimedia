HTML MULTIMEDIA

Use <img> for images, <audio> for sound, and <video> for moving pictures. The src attribute points to the media file. Give meaningful images descriptive alt text; use alt="" only when an image is purely decorative. Add controls so people can operate audio and video themselves.

Audio and video play once by default. Leave out the loop attribute when the media should stop at the end. Avoid autoplay, especially for sound. Captions can be provided for video with a <track> element. Keep media in clearly named folders and use paths relative to the HTML file.

For the blog assignment, combine text, images, audio, and video with useful headings and captions. Disabling replay after the first play requires JavaScript; plain HTML's default is to stop at the end while leaving the controls available.
